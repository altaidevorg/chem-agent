# Technical Specification: Advanced Chemical Reactor R-301

## 1. Overview
The R-301 is a state-of-the-art Continuous Stirred-Tank Reactor (CSTR) designed for high-pressure, high-temperature catalytic synthesis. It is constructed from Hastelloy C-276 to provide exceptional corrosion resistance in aggressive chemical environments.

## 2. Design Parameters
- **Working Volume**: 5,000 Liters
- **Design Pressure**: 150 bar (2,175 psi)
- **Design Temperature**: 450°C (842°F)
- **Material of Construction**: Hastelloy C-276 (Internal surfaces), Carbon Steel (External jacket)
- **Heating/Cooling**: Integrated dimple jacket for high-efficiency heat transfer.

## 3. Agitation System
- **Impeller Type**: Dual-stage pitched blade turbine for optimal mixing and gas dispersion.
- **Motor**: 75 kW (100 HP) variable speed drive (VSD).
- **Seal Type**: Double mechanical seal with pressurized barrier fluid system to prevent leakage.
- **Shaft Speed Range**: 0 - 250 RPM.

## 4. Instrumentation and Control
The R-301 is fully instrumented for automated control via the plant's Distributed Control System (DCS).
- **Temperature Control**: Multiple redundant PT100 sensors located at different heights within the reactor.
- **Pressure Control**: High-precision pressure transducers with integrated safety rupture discs.
- **Level Control**: Radar-based level transmitter for accurate volume monitoring.
- **pH/Conductivity**: In-line sensors for real-time monitoring of reaction progress.
- **Safety Interlocks**: Automated emergency shutdown (ESD) system triggered by high pressure, high temperature, or agitator failure.

## 5. Feeding and Discharge Systems
- **Inlet Ports**: Four independent inlet ports for liquid and gas feeds, equipped with mass flow controllers (MFCs).
- **Catalyst Loading**: Dedicated port for manual or automated catalyst addition under inert atmosphere.
- **Discharge Valve**: Bottom-mounted flush-seated valve to minimize dead zones and ensure complete drainage.
- **Sampling System**: Zero-dead-volume sampling valve for safe representative sampling during operation.

## 6. Safety Features
- **Rupture Discs**: Dual rupture discs with pressure monitoring in between to detect primary disc failure.
- **Relief Valves**: Sized for emergency venting in case of runaway reactions or external fire.
- **Nitrogen Purge**: Automated system for purging the reactor and maintaining an inert atmosphere.
- **Emergency Cooling**: Dedicated high-flow cooling water circuit independent of the main plant cooling system.

## 7. Performance Requirements
- **Mixing Efficiency**: Achieve 95% homogeneity within 120 seconds for standard viscosity fluids.
- **Heat Transfer Coefficient**: Minimum 500 W/m²·K during peak exothermic phases.
- **Corrosion Rate**: Less than 0.05 mm/year in specified process conditions.

## 8. Installation and Commissioning
- **Foundation**: Reinforced concrete pad designed for static and dynamic loads.
- **Piping Enclosure**: All process piping must be double-contained in high-risk areas.
- **Testing**: Hydrostatic testing at 1.5x design pressure and helium leak testing of all seals and connections.

## 9. Maintenance Schedule
- **Daily**: Visual inspection of seals, motor, and instrumentation.
- **Monthly**: Lubrication of agitator bearings and functional test of safety interlocks.
- **Annual**: Internal inspection of Hastelloy lining and replacement of mechanical seal wear parts.

## 10. Conclusion
The R-301 reactor is a critical asset for the facility, designed for maximum safety, reliability, and performance. Strict adherence to these specifications is required during procurement, installation, and operation.
