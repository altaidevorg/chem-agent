# 📊 Statistical Analysis Challenge (English)

This test evaluates the agent's ability to perform manual statistical calculations on a dataset.

## 📂 Dataset: `data/sensor_statistics.csv`
The file contains 10 temperature readings from sensor T-101.

## 🧪 Statistical Questions

1. **Mean (Average):** "Calculate the mean temperature for sensor T-101 in `data/sensor_statistics.csv`."
2. **Median:** "What is the median temperature value in the dataset? (Sort the values first)."
3. **Mode:** "Which temperature value appears most frequently (the mode)?"
4. **Range:** "What is the range of the temperature readings (Max - Min)?"
5. **Variance:** "Calculate the sample variance for these 10 readings. Show your step-by-step calculation (Sum of squared differences from the mean)."
6. **Standard Deviation:** "Based on the variance, what is the standard deviation of the temperature readings?"
