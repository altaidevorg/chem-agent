## Safety and Regulatory Update

Caffeine is a **monitored substance** by the **World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA)** in athletic competitions. While caffeine is not currently on WADA's Prohibited List at all times, it is subject to **threshold-based monitoring** at high concentrations. Athletes may face sanctions if their urine caffeine concentration exceeds **12 µg/mL** during competition testing. This threshold-based approach recognizes caffeine's widespread use in society while maintaining strict oversight in professional sports to ensure fair competition. The monitoring reflects caffeine's status as a psychoactive stimulant that can enhance performance, particularly in endurance and high-intensity activities.
